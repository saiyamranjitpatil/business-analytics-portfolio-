
# Revenue & Profitability Intelligence

### Business Analytics Case Study

A data-driven analysis of **sales performance, profitability, target achievement, and regional performance** using Python.

> **Dataset Note:** The original datasets used in this analysis were privately provided as part of an assessment and are not included in this repository. The repository contains the analytical workflow, derived outputs, visualizations, and business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand the underlying drivers of sales and profitability, evaluate target performance, and identify regional opportunities that can support better business decision-making.

The analysis focuses on three key areas:

* **Sales & Profitability** — Which categories generate the most sales and profit?
* **Target Achievement** — How are monthly sales targets changing over time?
* **Regional Performance** — Which states contribute the most orders, revenue, and profit?

---

## 🔍 Business Questions

### 01 — Sales & Profitability

* What is the total sales generated by each category?
* What is the average profit per order?
* What is the profit margin for each category?
* Which categories are performing strongly?
* Which categories require improvement?
* What could explain differences in category performance?

### 02 — Target Achievement

* How has the Furniture category's monthly sales target changed?
* Which months experienced significant target fluctuations?
* What patterns can be observed in target-setting trends?
* How can target expectations better align with historical performance?

### 03 — Regional Performance

* Which five states generate the highest number of orders?
* How much sales do these states generate?
* What is their average profitability?
* Which regions demonstrate strong sales but weaker profitability?
* Which regions should receive greater business attention?
